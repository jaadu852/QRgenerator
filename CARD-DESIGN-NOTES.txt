Card artwork update v5
The print card now uses the supplied reference photograph as the full-bleed artwork template, cropped to the card edges and scaled to exactly 101.6 x 101.6 mm. The old sample QR area is covered and replaced with each generated dynamic QR image. This preserves the reference typography, logo, gradient, waves, NFC phone illustration, feedback lettering and layout instead of approximating them with HTML text and symbols.
Blue and black choices are both available. Black variant is generated by recoloring the blue background elements in the reference artwork. A4 sheets remain 2 cards per page with a 5 mm gap.
Print using Actual Size / 100%, and disable browser headers/footers.
