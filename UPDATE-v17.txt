CloudKrafts QR Manager v17

Changes:
1. Increased QR overlay in the PDF from 20% to 23% of sticker width/height (23.69 mm on a 103 mm sticker). Repositioned within the logo's blue QR frame; QR ID moved below the larger code.
2. Added Search QR field and Search QR button in the admin dashboard.
3. Search by unique ID such as ck000123 opens a popup with current status, shop name and destination URL.
4. Admin can change status to Unused or Active and save.
5. Choosing Unused clears shop name, destination URL and management PIN so the QR can be activated again through the vendor activation flow.
6. Choosing Active requires a valid http/https URL; updates shop name and destination immediately.
7. Changes are protected by admin password and recorded in the audit log.

Keep logo.png and local-data.sqlite3 from your existing installation. Replace the old software files with this version, then run start-windows.bat.
